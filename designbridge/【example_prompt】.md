# 室內設計文字輸入範例集

## A 類：純風格 / 氛圍描述

A1. 我想要北歐極簡風的客廳，白色牆壁搭配淺木色家具，整體乾淨清爽，有大量自然光。

A2. 幫我設計一個日式侘寂風的臥室，大地色系、粗麻布料、低矮床架，安靜有禪意。

A3. 想要輕奢風格的餐廳，黑色鐵件餐桌搭配絲絨餐椅，大理石紋牆面，金色吊燈，有品味但不誇張。

A4. 我喜歡台式懷舊風，老木窗、復古磁磚地板、溫暖燈光，感覺有老台灣的人情味。

A5. 幫我打造一個有海島度假感的主臥，藍白色系，藤編床頭板，白色薄紗窗簾，像峇里島villa。

---

## B 類：功能需求導向

B1. 我需要一個書房，有大書桌、整面書牆和舒適的椅子，燈光要夠亮，適合長時間工作。

B2. 幫我設計給兩個小孩共用的房間，要有上下鋪床架，各自有獨立讀書區，收納要多。

B3. 我家廚房想改成開放式，搭配中島，方便下廚時和家人互動，要有足夠的備料空間。

B4. 需要一個寵物友善的客廳，角落有貓跳台和狗窩，地板用好清潔的材質，動線要順。

B5. 幫我把陽台改造成休閒區，放兩張椅子和茶几，可以喝茶看書，有點綠意。

---

## C 類：模糊情境描述

C1. 我最近工作壓力很大，想要一個回家就能立刻放鬆的客廳，不知道該怎麼設計。

C2. 我想要一個每天早上起來心情都很好的臥室，感覺要很舒服，但我說不清楚要什麼風格。

C3. 家裡感覺很亂、很壓迫，我也不知道問題在哪，就是希望看起來清爽一點。

C4. 我家住了五年還是感覺像剛搬進來，很空白、沒有溫度，但我不知道到底少了什麼。

C5. 我希望客廳的設計讓我一下班開門的瞬間就能感到放鬆。

---

## D 類：特殊情境 / 限制條件

D1. 我家只有十坪的套房，床、沙發、工作桌都要放進去，但不想感覺很擠，請幫我規劃。

D2. 老人要住的房間，家具不能有尖角，動線要寬敞好走，方便輪椅或助行器移動。

D3. 小孩房要安全又有趣，地板鋪軟墊，收納要小孩自己能使用的高度，顏色活潑但不刺眼。

D4. 出租套房的改造，預算有限，主要靠燈光和軟裝提升質感，讓房客住得舒適。

D5. 我家採光不好，只有一個小窗，請幫我設計讓空間感覺更明亮開闊的方案。

# Interior Design Prompt Examples (English)

## Category A: Style / Atmosphere

A1. I want a Nordic minimalist living room with white walls, light wood furniture, clean and airy, flooded with natural light.

A2. Design a Japanese wabi-sabi bedroom for me — earthy tones, rough linen fabrics, a low bed frame, quiet and zen.

A3. I'd like a refined luxury dining room: black iron table with velvet chairs, marble-textured walls, gold pendant lights — tasteful but not excessive.

A4. I love a vintage Taiwanese aesthetic — old wooden window frames, retro tile flooring, warm lighting, with a nostalgic homey feel.

A5. Create a tropical resort-style master bedroom: blue and white palette, rattan headboard, sheer white curtains — like a Bali villa.

---

## Category B: Function-Driven

B1. I need a home office with a large desk, a full wall of bookshelves, and a comfortable chair. Lighting should be bright enough for long work sessions.

B2. Design a shared bedroom for two kids with bunk beds, separate study corners for each, and plenty of storage.

B3. I want to convert my kitchen into an open-plan layout with an island, so I can interact with family while cooking. Needs enough prep space.

B4. I need a pet-friendly living room with a cat tree and dog bed in the corner, easy-to-clean flooring, and a smooth traffic flow.

B5. Transform my balcony into a relaxing nook — two chairs, a small coffee table, somewhere to read with a bit of greenery.

---

## Category C: Vague / Emotional Description

C1. Work has been really stressful lately. I want a living room where I can instantly unwind the moment I get home, but I'm not sure how to design it.

C2. I want a bedroom that makes me feel good every morning when I wake up — comfortable and uplifting, though I can't quite put a style to it.

C3. My home feels cluttered and oppressive, but I can't pinpoint why. I just want it to feel cleaner and more breathable.

C4. I've lived here five years and it still feels like I just moved in — blank, no warmth. I don't know what's missing.

C5. I want the living room design to make me feel relaxed the instant I open the door after work.

---

## Category D: Special Constraints

D1. My apartment is only 33 sqm and needs to fit a bed, sofa, and work desk without feeling cramped. Help me plan the layout.

D2. A room for an elderly person — no sharp furniture corners, wide clear pathways, easy to navigate with a wheelchair or walker.

D3. A safe and fun kids' room: soft floor padding, storage at child height, lively colors that aren't too stimulating.

D4. Renovating a rental unit on a tight budget — mainly improve the feel through lighting and soft furnishings to make it comfortable for tenants.

D5. My apartment has poor natural light with only one small window. Design a space that feels brighter and more open.
